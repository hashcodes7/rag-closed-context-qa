Helen Giza serves as the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and Chair of the Management Board of Fresenius Medical Care. She was appointed to the position in December 2022, having previously served as the company's Chief Financial Officer and Chief Transformation Officer.

The company's leadership structure also includes CEOs for its specific operating segments who serve on the Management Board:

* Craig Cordola: Appointed CEO of Care Delivery, effective January 1, 2024.
* Joseph E. (Joe) Turk: Appointed CEO of Care Enablement, effective January 1, 2026.
* Charles Hugh-Jones: Appointed Global Chief Medical Officer and member of the Management Board, effective January 1, 2026.

It is important to distinguish between Fresenius Medical Care and its parent company, Fresenius SE & Co. KGaA; Michael Sen serves as the CEO (Chairman of the Management Board) of the Fresenius Group.
