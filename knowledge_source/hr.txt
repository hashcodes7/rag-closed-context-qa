TechNova Human Resources Information:
- Annual Leave: Employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, plus 10 public holidays.
- Sick Leave: Up to 10 days of paid sick leave annually.
- Health Insurance: Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage provided through BlueCross.
- Retirement: 401(k) matching up to 6% of the base salary.
- Performance Reviews: Conducted bi-annually in June and December.
- Maternity/Paternity Leave: 16 weeks of fully paid leave for primary caregivers.
- Contact: For any HR related queries, email hr-support@technova.com.
