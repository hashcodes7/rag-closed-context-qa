Company Overview:
Cognizant is an American multinational information technology services and consulting company founded in 1994. Our headquarters is located in Teaneck, New Jersey, with global delivery centers worldwide.
Mission: To engineer modern businesses to improve everyday life. We help clients modernize technology, reimagine processes and transform experiences so they can stay ahead in our fast-changing world.
Key Services:
- Digital Transformation: Modernizing legacy IT infrastructure and enterprise applications.
- Cloud Computing: Cloud strategy, migration, and infrastructure management.
- Artificial Intelligence: Enterprise AI, generative AI solutions, and advanced data analytics.
