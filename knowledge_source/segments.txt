Fresenius Medical Care primarily operates within two main segments following its strategic reorganization: Care Delivery and Care Enablement.

### Care Delivery
The Care Delivery segment represents the company's vast network of dialysis clinics and patient care services worldwide. It is responsible for providing direct medical care to individuals suffering from end-stage renal disease (ESRD). This includes:
- In-center hemodialysis
- Home dialysis options (peritoneal dialysis and home hemodialysis)
- Related medical services, vascular care, and pharmacy services

### Care Enablement
The Care Enablement segment is the manufacturing and product-focused arm of the company. It develops, manufactures, and distributes the medical technologies and supplies necessary for dialysis treatment. Key products include:
- Hemodialysis machines
- Dialyzers (artificial kidneys)
- Bloodline systems
- Water treatment systems
- Peritoneal dialysis systems and solutions

### Strategic Transformation
In 2023, under the leadership of CEO Helen Giza, Fresenius Medical Care implemented a major transformation program to simplify its structure, increase agility, and improve capital allocation. The transition from a region-based matrix to the two global operating segments (Care Delivery and Care Enablement) was a core component of this effort to streamline operations and unlock value.
