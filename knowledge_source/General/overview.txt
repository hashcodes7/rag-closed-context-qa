Fresenius Medical Care is the world's leading provider of products and services for individuals with chronic kidney failure. The company operates across the entire dialysis value chain, offering a comprehensive range of solutions for both in-center and home dialysis.

### Company Overview
* Mission: To create a future worth living for patients worldwide by improving their quality of life through high-quality products, innovative technologies, and advanced treatment concepts.
* Global Reach: As of December 31, 2025, the company operated a global network of approximately 3,600 dialysis centers, caring for more than 292,000 patients.
* Operations: They manage around 35 production sites across all continents to manufacture essential dialysis equipment and supplies.
* Headquarters: Bad Homburg v. d. Höhe, Germany.

### History
* Origins: The company’s heritage is rooted in over 100 years of medical innovation, tracing back to a German pharmacy established in 1462 and the founding of Fresenius AG by Dr. Eduard Fresenius in 1912.
* Key Turning Point (1966): The company made the strategic decision to begin manufacturing dialysis products, which revolutionized the treatment of kidney failure.
* Formation (1996): Fresenius Medical Care was officially formed through the merger of Fresenius AG’s dialysis division and National Medical Care (NMC), a U.S.-based provider of dialysis services. This merger created a vertically integrated global leader.
* Growth & Expansion: Over the decades, the company has expanded through numerous strategic acquisitions—such as Renal Care Group, Liberty Dialysis Holdings, and American Access Care—and partnerships in pharmaceuticals and health IT.

### Products & Services
Fresenius Medical Care provides products and services designed for the entire renal care spectrum:
* Dialysis Products: The company manufactures a wide array of equipment, including dialysis machines, dialyzers, and related disposables.
* Dialysis Services: They provide comprehensive care through their global network of dialysis centers, supporting both in-center and home dialysis treatment options.
* Renal Pharmaceuticals & Solutions: Through ventures and acquisitions, the company has expanded into renal pharmaceuticals (e.g., phosphate binders), laboratory services, and nephrology-specific electronic health records (e.g., Acumen nEHR).
* Research & Development: R&D remains a core pillar, with a focus on creating affordable, high-quality therapies and innovative medical technologies.
